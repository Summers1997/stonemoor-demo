Driveways

Resin, block paving and tarmac

Three surfaces, three price brackets, three reasons to pick one over another. Here is the honest version, including when we would tell you not to bother.

Resin bound

A stone aggregate mixed with clear resin and trowelled over a solid base. Smooth, permeable, and the finish most people picture when they say they want a modern drive.

It suits you if your existing base is sound, you want no weeds and no loose stone, and you are happy in the £70–£110 per square metre bracket.

It does not suit you if the base is broken up. Resin is only as good as what is underneath it, and laying it over a failing base is the most common way this job goes wrong. If yours needs digging out we will say so, and it will change the price.

Block paving

Individual blocks laid on sand over a compacted sub-base. The most forgiving surface: if a service needs digging up in five years, the blocks lift and go back down and nobody can tell.

Expect £60–£95 per square metre depending on the block. Budget for re-sanding the joints every few years — it is a ten-minute job and it is what keeps the weeds out.

Tarmac

The cheapest per square metre and by far the fastest to lay. Best for long farm or field access where appearance matters less than getting a vehicle over it in February.

How long it takes

A typical two-car drive is three to five working days: a day to dig out, a day for the sub-base, a day to lay, and a day either side for weather. We do not start a job we cannot finish, so if the forecast is against us we will move you rather than leave a hole.

What we will not do

We will not lay a new surface over a base we do not trust, and we will not quote for a job we think somebody closer to you should do more cheaply. Both of those cost us work and save you money, which is roughly the point.

What we guarantee — and what we don’t

Two guarantees, with their limits written down. If a term below is not clear, ask before you accept a quote rather than after.

10 year structural guarantee

The base, the sub-base and the drainage

Sinking, heaving, cracking or standing water caused by how the ground was prepared. If the base fails, we take the surface up, put the base right and relay it at our cost.

Insurance-backed. Underwritten by the scheme named on the live site, so the cover stands even if we are no longer trading. Your certificate is issued within 30 days of the final invoice — keep it with your deeds.
